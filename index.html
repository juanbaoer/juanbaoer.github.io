Research

My research focuses on the application of generative models to problems in soft matter physics, with a particular interest in the simulation of supercooled liquids.

We are interested in how modern generative models can be used to improve the sampling and exploration of complex configurational spaces in soft matter systems, especially in glass-forming and supercooled liquids.
